🔗 Connect your Google Sheet गूगल शीट जोड़ें

Without a connection, orders stay safely on this computer only (demo mode). Connect your sheet once, and every order also lands in your Google Sheet — readable from anywhere.

How to connect — 5 minutes कैसे जोड़ें

  1. Open your Google Sheet, then click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete whatever is in the editor, paste the entire code below, then press Ctrl+S to save.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment. Next to "Select type", choose Web app.
  4. Set "Execute as" to Me, and "Who has access" to Anyone. Click Deploy, allow access when asked.
  5. Copy the Web app URL (it ends with /exec), paste it in the box above, press Save, then Test connection.
  6. Tip: open the /exec URL with ?action=list at the end in Chrome — you should see your orders as text. That means it works.

The script creates these columns by itself the first time it runs:
Timestamp | OrderID | CustomerName | Phone | CakeType | Size | MessageOnCake | PickupDate | TotalPrice | AdvancePaid | Balance | Status | Notes
